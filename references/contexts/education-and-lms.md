## Brand intent

Education motion should communicate learning, progress, access, guidance, or achievement. Avoid using childish bounce, fake progress, or generic “AI” effects as a substitute for a meaningful concept.

## EDU-01 — Concept-to-symbol

Use one metaphor such as a book, page, compass, pathway, or opening door and transform it into the stable mark. Prefer a compatible morph or a simple settle; do not stack several metaphors.

**Timing:** 1.0–1.7 s; metaphor 0–800 ms, transformation 350–1250 ms, hold.

**Layers:** metaphor silhouette, core mark, page/grid line, wordmark.

**QA:** the intended concept should be identifiable to the target audience, and the final mark must remain unchanged.

## EDU-02 — Progress to completion

Draw a progress ring to its true value, then reveal a check only when the product state is genuinely complete.

**Timing:** 0.9–1.6 s; progress 0–1000 ms, check 700–1300 ms.

**Safety:** never imply completion when application data says incomplete. Provide a static state and accessible text equivalent.

## EDU-03 — Knowledge network

Use three to five nodes to connect to a central symbol, then simplify or remove the construction network.

**Timing:** 1.2–2.0 s; nodes 0–700 ms, links 300–1300 ms, simplification 900–1600 ms.

**Risks:** spaghetti geometry, childish tone, competing with course content.

## EDU-04 — Compact LMS state

Use a 90–150 ms response for active, success, or compact-header state. Reserve the full logo animation for onboarding, milestones, or major transitions.

**QA:** repeated use must not distract learners or delay navigation. Keyboard focus and non-motion state must remain clear.

## Timing and restraint

Education brands often benefit from a clear sequence: establish, connect or progress, resolve, hold. Keep the wordmark readable and avoid decorative particles that resemble data or achievement without meaning.

## QA

- Match visual state to actual product state.
- Test reduced motion and a static completion state.
- Check text and symbols at mobile size.
- Confirm the animation does not obscure course content or calls to action.
- Preserve language, accessibility, and screen-reader meaning outside the motion.
