## Energy control

Playful motion is not an excuse to deform the identity. Pick one expressive gesture, give it a clear beginning and end, and let the logo return to its exact canonical state.

## PLAY-01 — Spring reveal

Enter at 88–94% scale and settle through one 4–8% overshoot.

**Timing:** 0.6–1.1 s; entrance 0–500 ms, overshoot 350–700 ms, hold.

**Rules:** one overshoot only; no independent bounce on every wordmark letter; no deformation of counters or hairline letter parts.

## PLAY-02 — Squash and stretch

Apply 2–4% temporary deformation only to an approved soft form or mascot body.

**Timing:** 0.8–1.3 s; anticipation up to 100 ms, deformation 100–650 ms, return 450–1000 ms.

**Risks:** physics feels arbitrary, logo geometry appears altered, reduced-motion mismatch.

## PLAY-03 — Gesture or celebration

Use one recognizable gesture: wave, nod, page turn, or a small confetti burst after the logo is readable.

**Timing:** 1.2–2.2 s; reveal 0–900 ms, gesture or burst 700–1700 ms, hold.

**Limits:** no continuous gesture loop unless it is the intended brand behavior; maximum one celebration burst; no rapid luminance changes.

## QA

- Confirm the final state is geometrically exact.
- Inspect the overshoot at actual size and on small screens.
- Test whether the personality reads without becoming childish or cheap.
- Provide a reduced-motion version with the same message.
- Check that sound, if any, does not create a misleading product state.
