## Design posture

Premium motion is usually about control, timing, and material restraint. Use one gesture, a clean endpoint, and enough hold for the viewer to trust the identity. Do not add a glow because the logo is blue, and do not add a camera move because the canvas is empty.

## PREM-01 — Hairline trace

Reveal the mark with one controlled line and subtle opacity. Keep hairlines visible at the actual delivery size.

**Timing:** 1.4–2.2 s; trace 0–1500 ms; hold at least 700 ms.

**Layers:** primary outline, secondary detail, wordmark, optional mask.

## PREM-02 — Quiet lockup

Introduce the symbol with a short fade/translate, then settle the wordmark with minimal tracking change.

**Timing:** 1.0–1.8 s; symbol 0–700 ms, wordmark 400–1300 ms, hold.

**Forbidden shortcuts:** generic bounce, particles, repeated camera zoom, excessive metallic sweep, blur, or unstable tracking.

## PREM-03 — Restrained foil sweep

Only for campaign delivery. Keep the canonical logo readable and separate the highlight as an optional effect layer.

**Timing:** one 400–800 ms pass after the logo is already readable; total sequence preferably under 2.8 s.

## QA

- Check fine strokes and counters at small size.
- Verify no bloom, halo, banding, or color shift alters the mark.
- Confirm the final frame is identical to the approved master.
- Test on white, near-white, black, and the intended brand background.
- Ask whether the motion would still make sense without sound.
