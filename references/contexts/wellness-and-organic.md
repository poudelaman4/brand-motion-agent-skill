# Wellness and Organic Context

## Intent

Use motion to communicate calm, care, continuity, breath, balance, or natural growth. The mark should feel alive without becoming restless.

## Recommended patterns

- Organic unfurl with root-based pivots
- Slow 1–2% breathing loop with a rest point
- Quiet tracking settle for a wordmark
- Single highlight pass only when the mark remains canonical

## Timing

Favor 1.6–4.0 seconds for a one-shot organic reveal and 4–8 seconds for a loop. Use clean `organic` or `enter` easing, long holds, and no rapid flashing.

## Risks

- Decorative motion that distracts from a calm interface
- Excessive particles, glitter, or neon
- A loop that never rests
- Reduced-motion users receiving the same large movement

## QA

Check the animation at actual interface size, on light and dark backgrounds, with reduced motion, and after repeated playback. Confirm the final state is recognizable without audio or motion.
