## Taste is a constraint set

Separate the three things called taste. Only two can be enforced against a file.

| | Principle | Rule | Preference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form | A generative claim about why choices are good | A checkable constraint | A trained reaction, no articulated cause |
| Test | Generate an unwritten rule | Show the file, pass or fail | Name what would prove it wrong |
| Failure | Inconsistent rules when vague | Brittle when cut from principle | Unteachable, often wrong |

Expect the common failure: a preference in a principle's clothes. "Bounce feels cheap" is a preference, wrong as stated — a consumer identity may run off a wink. Correct it:

```text
Overshoot claims the object has a physical body.
Permit only where a playfulness claim is documented and views run under 10 monthly.
```

Run the referent test on every move: name the physical, structural, historical, or semiotic referent, or reject the move as decoration.

## The register table

Read the five dimensions as gradable positions on a 0–5 scale, never binary switches: velocity is travel per second, force implied mass, angularity 0 (curved) to 5 (cornered), fluidity path continuity, expansiveness growth into or contraction from frame. Treat duration, easing, and volume as `[UNVERIFIED]` synthesis.

## Register judgements

**Premium/minimal.** Fit one heavy move, hard stop, from scale 0.94. Cheapen: a second gesture. Pace: 1.0–1.2 s. Failure: particle field proving restraint. Trap: PREM-03's foil sweep, a separate layer only.

**Heritage/craft.** Fit citation, not effect, as claimed material. Cheapen: digital artefacts, easing, stagger. Pace: one drawn pass, 1.5–3.0 s. Failure: glitch, announcing a startup. Trap: constant-velocity draw-on reads as a wipe.

**Technology/engineering.** Fit structure, routing, modular logic; report state, not performance. Cheapen: organic easing, blob, soft focus. Pace: 0.8–1.0 s per TECH-01. Failure: generic node assembly. Trap: animating connections the product lacks.

**Playful/character.** Fit elastic overshoot, secondary action, anticipation. Cheapen: solemn tempo, long build, sterile grid. Pace: 0.6–2.0 s, exact geometry. Failure: overshoot on function. Trap: cap identity geometry at PLAY-01's 4–8% per `contexts/playful-and-character.md`.

**Wellness/organic.** Fit breath-paced continuity, loops seamless by periodicity. Cheapen: any cut, contrast spike, seam. Pace: one slow move or a 4–8 s loop. Failure: a template blur. Trap: easing a gesture reads as sedation.

**Corporate/institutional.** Fit a short ease-out or a fade; the job is orientation. Cheapen: effects, sound, 3D, length. Pace: 1.0 s, hard stop. Failure: sound on a pension fund, a competence cue. Trap: over-smoothing is hesitation.

**Energetic/sport.** Fit high velocity, hard ends, angular travel. Cheapen: slow dissolve, lingering. Pace: 0.5–0.8 s ending on a hard cut. Failure: a 3 s cinematic intro. Trap: motion-trail streaks, unowned.

**Bold/luxury.** Fit amplitude, not duration: one large, slow move. Cheapen: speed, a second move, any addition. Pace: one move ≤1.5 s. Failure: depth and specular as costume. Trap: a small move taken slowly is hesitation.

**Friendly/family.** Fit low mass, real overshoot, generous holds, irregularity. Cheapen: cold precision, dead stops, even stagger. Pace: 2.0 s, ≤2 moves. Failure: mechanical evenness reads as institutional. Trap: legible as a sibling's, resemblance running backwards.

**Technical/precision.** Fit cornered, stepped, near-instant; blur or a hard cut replaces easing. Cheapen: elasticity, wobble, float. Pace: 0.6 s near-linear, per TECH-04 in `contexts/technology-and-software.md`. Failure: floating blur, the film-about-technology look. Trap: blur hiding an off-master landing.

**Editorial/cultural.** Build on type and grid, not the logo. Cheapen: chrome, sparkle, 3D, gloss. Pace: 2.5 s, 2–3 type-driven moves. Failure: a mark animating against headline type. Trap: fixing a thumbnail problem with the logo.

Apply the directional convention before ranking: up and to the right is the default growth trajectory audiences read, so a brand claiming permanence, heritage, or institutional authority must spend effort against it.

## Budget by frequency

Apply the frequency multiplier first: the most reliable restraint mechanism, needing no taste judgement. Begin motion within 0.1 s of the user action or the sense of direct manipulation is lost, and keep exits shorter than entrances.

| Expected views per person | Motion budget | Easing | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Once a year | Unlimited expression | Any curve | Launch ident |
| Once a session | Expressive allowed | Custom curve, overshoot | Onboarding |
| Daily | Restrained, 100–200 ms | Ease-out only | Page transition |
| Dozens a day | Effectively none | Instant or ≤100 ms | Hover, list |
| Keyboard-initiated | Never animate | None | Repeat action |

Take the tighter of the frequency row and the register ceiling; treat a fade as correct past 20 views monthly.

## Register conflicts

Let the register win when the structure supports a technique it forbids, unless the structural fact is the brand claim: a cite-able structure ships, anything else is a note for another project. Cut the technique, never the register, when the source cannot support it.

1. Stated brand claim. Nothing overrides it.
2. Frequency budget. Take the tighter of two.
3. Register ceiling on duration, volume, easing.
4. Structural feasibility of the source.
5. Default convention, including up-and-right.
6. Reference work. Imitation justifies nothing.

Write the live conflict into the brief with both sides named, then record the resolution as considered-and-rejected. A conflict resolved silently returns on the next revision.

## Limits

Ask rather than infer when no stated claim exists, when two registers fit the evidence equally, or when the sample is smaller than the deployment. Treat premium against bold and luxury, and technology against technical precision, as genuine pairs.

Separate the aspirational register from the actual one. A twenty-month-old company animating with a two-hundred-year-old gravity borrows authority it cannot document; name the target and the gap, and hold the piece to the lower.

Confirm every register against a claim quoted from the positioning, guidelines, or founder. Reject mood words: "premium" and "confident" can be neither checked nor improved, and two readers build two different pieces.

## QA

- Reject any move whose referent cannot be named in one sentence; count survivors against the volume ceiling.
- Measure the piece against the tighter of the duration ceiling and the frequency row.
- Reject the register's fatal move outright; it has no permitted dose, not a small one.
- Play the piece five times at real speed; require a neutral fifth viewing.
- Composite another brand's mark; reject when the motion transfers unchanged.
- Reject unexplained up-and-right drift on a permanence, heritage, or institutional brief.

Register values are `observed` from platform specifications and `inferred` from cited guidelines, not measured across delivered systems; hold the table as `provisional`.
